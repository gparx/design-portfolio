## Professional Introduction

My name is Grant Harrison Parker, I am currently a junior in mechanical engineering at The University of North Carolina at Charlotte. The reason I chose to become a mechanical engineer was to help bring humanity closer towards deep space exploration and colonization. There are many kinds of engineers and even more subcategories of those engineers. Some engineers are designers and more technical, while others are more theoretical and creative. I like to identify myself as someone who is more theoretical and creative, someone that challenges what's possible. 

Continuing my education I will expand my knowledge and expertise on mechanics, engineering design, problem solving and physics. My long term goals consist of pursuing my master degree in engineering and possibly even my PhD. My long term interests are focused around aerospace engineering and astronautical engineering, with my main focus revolving around pushing the theoretical limits of technology and mechanics to pursue deep space exploration. As time progresses I am becoming an engineer that does not only want to understand how something works, but also how it could work differently, more efficiently or more reliably. In conclusion my ultimate goal is to advance humanity closer to greatness.

## Defending an Engineering Decision

**What does it mean to defend an engineering decision — and do you currently know how to do it?**

To defend an engineering decision is to accept the possibility of failure, but also the effects of that failure. The effects of that failure can range from losing a company money, ruining your reputation to even catastrophic human casualties. All of these possibilities must be considered when defending an engineering decision. As for "do you currently know how to do it?" The answer is no, I do not know how to currently do it.
